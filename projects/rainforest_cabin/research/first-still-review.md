# First still review

Inspected the actual 1920x1080 Cycles output, including full-size detail.
The visual reference remains the image supplied in chat; no local reference file
was available for a pixel-aligned side-by-side.

## Present

- Sheltered porch framing on the left; timber veranda, front gable, small entry gable.
- Warm lantern against blue storm light; layered forest mist.
- Scanned ferns/broadleaf plants/rocks; original foreground palm fronds.
- Visible layered rain in the finished still; continuous reflective stream.

## Gaps against the reference and realism target

- Background trunks are too straight and uniform; some lack convincing bark relief.
- Timber joints, roof edges and steps remain too regular and clean. Add selective
  damage, wet staining, moss and more natural construction variation.
- Procedural palms repeat leaflet spacing/shape and read as synthetic at close range.
  They need better curvature/asymmetry and more convincing leaf detail.
- Creek has reflections but lacks small cascades, turbulent breakup and splash detail.
- Rain is visible, but thin straight streaks need softer exposure variation and
  convincing impacts in the motion test. Eave strands/ripple rings are static.
- The porch side wall is less prominent than in the reference; the scene is more open.
- Overall blue lighting is strongly graded; the final photographic balance needs review.

This is the first still milestone, not the final ultra-realistic scene. Review framing
and mood, then address the material/geometry gaps before an expensive long animation.
