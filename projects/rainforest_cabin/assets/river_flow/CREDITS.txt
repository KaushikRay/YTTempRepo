Prepared asset: real_rapids.mp4, source interval 70–78 s, silent, 24 fps frame
selection, H.264 CRF14. No synthetic frame interpolation. Source attribution is
retained voluntarily; provider licence metadata and checksums are in source.json.

Blender uses selected water-only footage regions to modulate moving photographic
water textures. It does not composite an unmodified rectangular video over the
scene. Banks and rocks remain from the generated scene plate. This method is not
a liquid fluid simulation or a camera photograph of the complete fictional scene.
