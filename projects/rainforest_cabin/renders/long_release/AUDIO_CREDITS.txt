LONG-VERSION PROCESSING
The 3 h and 8 h masters use randomized 63-second grains from valvalion's full rain track and Extemporalist's full flowing-water recording, with three-second equal-power overlaps, filtering, gain adjustment, occasional left/right swapping before spatial placement, and soft peak control. Bidgee's thunder is reused as four processed timing/pitch variations with sparse event scheduling. The scene fades to black after one hour in the eight-hour version while sound continues. No visual lightning is added.
